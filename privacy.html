Privacy Policy

RegX Mail Reader is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner.

It requests read-only access (gmail.readonly) to read verification code emails in the owner's own Gmail accounts.

Data is processed locally on the owner's computer. No email content is stored on any server, shared, sold or transferred to third parties.

Access can be revoked anytime at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
